Luxury Vehicle Transport

A Complete Guide to Shipping Luxury and Exotic Vehicles

Marcus Thorne Oct 28, 2026 5 min read

When shipping a Lamborghini, a restored vintage Porsche, or a brand-new Rolls-Royce, standard transport methods simply won't suffice. High-value vehicles require specialized logistics.

The Equipment Matters

For exotic cars, ground clearance is the biggest obstacle. Standard ramps on open carriers approach at too steep an angle, which can destroy a front splitter or undercarriage. Luxury carriers utilize hydraulic lift gates that keep the vehicle perfectly horizontal as it is lifted into the trailer.

Hard-Sided Enclosed Trailers

Luxury vehicles should exclusively travel in hard-sided enclosed trailers. This provides a clean environment, protecting the paint from rocks, road salt, and harsh sunlight.

Insurance Considerations

Standard carriers carry around $100,000 to $250,000 in cargo insurance. For exotics, this isn't enough. Dedicated luxury transport carriers hold insurance policies of $1 million or more, ensuring your asset is fully protected.

Communication

When paying a premium for white-glove service, you should expect premium communication. Drivers of luxury carriers are typically seasoned professionals who provide regular updates and discreet handling.
